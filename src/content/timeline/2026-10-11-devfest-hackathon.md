Built PitchCheck in a team of four: an AI tool that scores startup pitches (audio + slides) and gives feedback using Gemma 4, with a FastAPI backend and a Next.js frontend. Scoring follows a rubric based on Y Combinator's pitch advice and runs fully on-premise, so pitches stay private.
